# Structure of the results so far

The principle under test (wording to fix with Pedro; `syntheses/narratives/
intro-narrative.md`): a probabilistic theory is compatible with spacetime of
dimension d if it can be the target of a d-dimensional topological field
theory, a symmetric monoidal functor Bord_d → (GPT, ⊗) with the theory's
systems on slices [T1–T6; A6]. Dimension one asks for dualizable systems;
dimension two asks for a commutative Frobenius algebra on the ring's system
with all four structure maps processes. Unitarity for GPT-valued theories is a
DECISION owed (Result 1 uses "dagger in CPM").

## Result 0. A theory without dualizable systems fails in every dimension

*Boxworld, every ⊗_min or ⊗_max theory with a non-classical system, and the
regular polygons under local tomography admit no topological field theory in
any dimension.* File: `results-narrative-1d.md` §1–3.

**Statement.** If a system X of a GPT is not dualizable, no TFT of any
dimension has X on a slice. Boxworld has no dualizable system at all.

**Steps.**

0.1 *Slice reduction.* Crossing with a slice E embeds Bord_1 into Bord_n; Bord_1
is generated by the point, its reverse and the two bends, subject to the snake
relation; so Z(E) is dualizable in the target for every slice of every theory
[CR Thm 3.4, Lemma 2.4, Prop 2.6; T3, T5]. ✓ (literature)

0.2 *The dual is forced* (Theorem A of the 1d file; assumes V_AB = V_A ⊗ V_B
for the composites involved, X1). X = (V, V⁺, u) is dualizable iff
ψ = Σ_a f_a ⊗ f^a is a state of X ⊗ X^∨ and an effect on X^∨ ⊗ X, where
X^∨ = (V*, (V⁺)*, u^∨). Proof: (a) the forgetful functor to Vec_R is faithful
and strong monoidal, duality data in Vec_R are unique up to unique isomorphism,
so the linear data are ψ and evaluation; (b) cap positive on product states
forces (V^∨)⁺ ⊂ (V⁺)*, cup positive on product effects forces the reverse;
(c) conversely the snake identities hold in Vec_R, hence in GPT. ✓ [R1, R2
verified in the 1d file; C7]

0.3 *The snake is exact teleportation, chains are entanglement swapping* [C8,
R3; BBLW Thm 1; DG Eq. 2]. Context for the reading, not load-bearing.

0.4 *Separable duality data are measure-and-prepare* (Theorem C of the 1d
file). If the cap is Σ_i c_i g_i ⊗ f_i with g_i states of X and f_i effects of
X, the snake sends x to Σ_i c_i f_i(x) g_i; likewise for a separable cup. So
id_X is a measure-and-prepare channel. ✓

0.5 *The identity is measure-and-prepare only for classical systems* [G18;
P Thm 7.7, Cor 7.8]. Self-contained proof in the 1d file: the preparation
states must include every extreme ray, the measuring effects then separate the
extreme rays, so the extreme rays are linearly independent and the cone is a
simplex. ✓

0.6 *Boxworld* (Theorem D of the 1d file). Boxworld composites are ⊗_max for
states [E7; B §VI], so by min–max duality [G12] every bipartite effect is
separable. A dualizable gbit would have a separable cap, hence by 0.4–0.5 be
classical; its state space is a square. So no gbit, and no composite of gbits,
is dualizable, and boxworld has no TFT in any dimension. Generalisation: a
non-classical system needs entangled states and entangled effects on its
composite with its dual; ⊗_max theories lack the second, ⊗_min theories the
first. Boxworld has PR boxes and no annihilation effect, the trade-off of
Short–Barrett [0909.2601, TARGET file]. ✓

0.7 *Loop weights* (Theorem B of the 1d file; composite-independent under
X1). If X is dualizable and V_AB = V_A ⊗ V_B, then tr(g) ≥ 0 for every
process g : X → X, because the closed worldline carrying g is a morphism
1 → 1, a nonnegative scalar, and equals the linear trace once the forgetful
functor is strong monoidal. Consequences: the gbit's double bit flip has trace
−1, so no locally tomographic composite makes the gbit dualizable; regular
n-gons with n ≥ 4 and rotations contain a rotation with 1 + 2cos θ < 0. Real
quantum theory is not locally tomographic, so the rebit's trace −1 on Sym² is
not an obstruction (its composite has the global sector; Result 2). ✓ REVIEW

0.8 *Which theories have cups and caps.* Classical (the perfectly correlated
pair; separable data are fine because the system is classical), quantum
(Choi–Jamiołkowski), real quantum (Bell state in Sym(W ⊗ W)), OST (the twisted
Bell pair, O10), the Dmello–Gross families by construction [C13]. ✓

## Result 1. Quantum theory satisfies the principle in dimension two

*The two-dimensional TFTs valued in the GPT of quantum theory on a qudit are
exactly those of FHilb; unitary corresponds to dagger bijectively.* File:
`results-quantum-2dUTFT.md`.

**Steps.**

1.1 The three categories and Q ≅ CPM(FHilb) (Lemma 1.4); the doubling functor
D(f) = f(·)f†, dagger strong symmetric monoidal (1.5). ✓
1.2 2d TFTs are commutative Frobenius algebras; unitary ones in FHilb are
orthonormal bases with weights; Euler theories E_λ (§2). ✓ (literature: CR,
Sawin, CPV, DJ)
1.3 Theorem A(i): D∘Z is a 2d TFT in CPM(FHilb), dagger if Z unitary. ✓
1.4 Theorem A(ii): every 2d TFT in CPM(FHilb) is a doubling. Lemmas 4.1–4.6:
unit pure (identity channel has Kraus rank one), counit pure, cup and cap pure,
multiplication of Kraus rank one by the associativity identity, lift is
Frobenius. ✓
1.5 Theorem A(iii),(iv): the fibre of D is the unit-modulus Euler theories;
unitary ↔ dagger with no residual ambiguity (§5; uses Schneider 1965 for
"isomorphisms in CPM are conjugations", TARGET file or replace). ✓
1.6 Theorem A(v): explicit doubled structure maps, Z_Q(Σ_g) = (Σ_i θ_i^{1−g})²,
Z_Q(T²) = N² (§6). ✓
1.7 Theorem B: the real Frobenius algebra on the ambient space is
Herm_N ≅ R^N ⊕ C^{N(N−1)/2}, β of signature (N(N+1)/2, N(N−1)/2) (§7). ✓
1.8 Remarks: dimension one recovered; non-dagger theories are real; scalars;
the Euler phases are the Kraus phases made gluing-consistent; the template for
a general GPT (§8). ✓

## Result 2. Real quantum theory satisfies the principle in dimension two

*Same argument: real quantum theory is CPM(FHilb_R).* File:
`results-quantum-2dUTFT.md` §9 (Theorem A′).

**Steps.**

2.1 Real quantum theory defined: systems (Sym_N(R), PSD_N(R), tr), composite
Sym(W ⊗ W') = Sym(W) ⊗ Sym(W') ⊕ Λ²W ⊗ Λ²W' (global sector; local tomography
fails); processes are Kraus maps, forced because a map on Sym(W) alone has no
action on the sector (9.1). ✓ (convention; DECISION 10.8 owed)
2.2 Lemma 9.2: Q^R ≅ CPM(FHilb_R); a Kraus map is fixed by its values on
symmetric rank-ones. The isomorphism does not factor through Vec_R. ✓
2.3 Theorem A′: (i)–(v) transfer. Fibre is a sign, E_{±1} ≅ trivial, so the
bijection has no ambiguity at all; unitary = transpose-dagger; the purity
lemmas use only positivity, rank and Kraus identities, valid over R. Step 5.2
needs the real analogue of Schneider (TARGET 10.6). ✓ modulo 10.6
2.4 Remark 9.4: over R, unitary theories are real C*-algebras R^k ⊕ C^m, more
than bases; the C-summand case is TARGET.
2.5 Remark 9.5: where it differs. The copants has a component in Λ²W ⊗ Λ²W;
the ambient algebra on the full square End(W) is R^{N²} with no complex
summands; the locally tomographic truncation is not positive (eigenvalue −1/2
for N = 2); the C-summands of complex QT are i × the antisymmetric directions
real QT excludes. For the template: the ambient space of a non-locally-
tomographic theory is the full square. ✓

## Result 3. Oblate stabilizer theory: dimension one yes, dimension two no

*OST is dualizable, but none of the unitary 2d TFTs of its ambient space
Herm(C²), the doubled qubit theories, restricts to it.* File:
`results-ost-2dUTFT.md`; context `syntheses/toolbox/example-ost.md`.

**Steps.**

3.1 OST defined with its subtleties (O1–O13): same ambient space as the qubit,
octahedral cones offset by π/4, r = 2^{1/4}, eight twisted Bell generators,
processes completely positive on states, effects = completely positive
functionals (so the restricted effect cone is derived, not postulated). ✓
3.2 Dimension one holds: cup 4Φ_R, cap Φ_R, snake identity (O10). ✓
3.3 Lemma 2.1 (general): the Frobenius form of any GPT-valued 2d TFT is a
linear isomorphism of the state cone onto the effect cone; the handle is a
state; the state cone is closed under the pants. ✓
3.4 Lemma 2.2: OST's admissible forms are the four twisted Bell forms
Φ_{R^k}, the Bell forms of the real structures at azimuth k·22.5°; signature
(3, 1). ✓ by exact LP on eight candidates (O13); hand proof TARGET.
3.5 Corollary 2.3: C ⊕ C excluded; R² ⊕ C needs positive real weights; R⁴
needs a negative weight and is not unitary. Under unitarity the algebra is
quantum-type. ✓
3.6 Theorem 3.1, the doubled theories: unit and counit coincide and must be a
pole (only pure states in both octahedra); the cup is the Bell state of the
basis and must be Φ_R, so the basis is a real basis rotated by 22.5°; the
handle is a pure state and lies in the octahedron only for equal weights. One
candidate remains up to symmetry: basis R^{1/2}|±⟩, unit and handle z̃₊, cup
OST's own teleportation resource. ✓
3.7 The pants fails on it: the gbit vertices have entries ½(1 ± r cos π/8) =
1.049, −0.049 in that basis, and μ(x̃₊ ⊗ x̃₋) = −((√2−1)/(2κ)) z̃₋, a negative
multiple of a state; the same on the effect side. One inequality,
r cos(π/8) = 1.0987 > 1. ✓ [num ✓ both frames]
3.8 Lemma 4.1: for any nonzero offset 2φ between state and effect frames with
the gbit normalisation r² cos 2φ = 1, the half-twist basis entry
½(1 − cos φ/√cos 2φ) is negative; only strong self-duality escapes. ✓
3.9 Beyond doublings: non-doubled R² ⊕ C algebras with the Bell form (and R⁴
without unitarity) are open; all positivity conditions are linear in
T(x,y,z) = β(xy,z), so an exact LP decides the polyhedral part (TARGET);
dominance lemma 5.2 available; random searches found nothing [num].
