# pipeline

Questions and ideas on their way to results. Future tense, live.

In a one-paper repository the pipeline holds the sub-questions the paper must
answer, and the ideas that might become its results or a follow-up. The stages
and their costs are the same as in a group repository; what changes is that
"project" means "in the draft, or spun out into its own repository".

```
1. IDEA      in someone's head                          cost: 0
2. ENTRY     one paragraph in pipeline/<topic>.md       cost: minutes
3. WORKUP    same file, sections fill in over weeks     cost: weeks   <- the big part
4. PROPOSED  ready to be written into the draft         cost: an afternoon
5. PROJECT   in the draft (B9), or its own repository
```

Entering stays nearly free. Writing into the draft stays a real decision.
Sections fill in incrementally; an empty section shows where the next
contribution goes. Disagreement goes under "Open questions and disagreements"
rather than being smoothed away.

## The results

`draft.tex` has a placeholder for results (B9) and none stated. The earlier
technical notes that produced results were retired on 2026-09-04 (git tag
`archive/pre-reorg-260904`). Results re-enter here as pipeline items, so that
their assumptions get written into `conventions/domain/` before they are
written into the draft.

Agents do not create pipeline files on their own initiative; they note the
idea in a commit message or a synthesis and Pedro decides. The three files
here on 2026-09-04 were created on Pedro's instruction from what the draft
already contains.
