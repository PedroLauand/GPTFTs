## Rules

**Pedro decides what lands here.** Agents write on a branch; the diff is the
review.

**Cite into `sources/` rather than restating.** For the draft's own argument,
cite the beat label in `paper/draft.tex`.

**Mark what isn't known** with `UNVERIFIED:` inline. A gap costs less than an
unmarked guess.

**Report contradictions rather than resolving them.**

Front matter carries `status`, `last-reviewed`, `sources` and
`machine-written`. See `conventions/meta/file-conventions.md`.

Everything here was rebuilt from `paper/draft.tex` alone on 2026-09-04 and is
machine-written. Nothing cites a filed source yet, because none is filed.
