# Toolbox: axioms in the GPT literature

The structural conditions a GPT may satisfy, in house notation, with the source
statement and what is known to imply what. Reconstruction axiom lists (Hardy,
CDP, Masanes–Müller) are in `conditions-catalogue.md`; which examples satisfy
which condition is the table in `gpt-examples.md`. Labels X1–X22 are stable.

## Composition

**X1. Tomographic locality.** V_AB = V_A ⊗ V_B: joint states are fixed by local
statistics. [G11]

**X2. No-signalling.** Marginals independent of remote settings; automatic in
any composite. [G13]

**X3. Causality, terminality.** One deterministic effect u_A per system. [C5]

**X4. No-restriction.** E_A = [0, u_A]. Generalised form: if a process gives
positive scalars on all states, effects and ancillas it is physically possible;
cups and caps imply it. [G7; JL Thm 1; SSC Lemma 4.10]

**X5. Entangleability.** (V_A^+, V_B^+) entangleable iff both non-classical;
otherwise nuclear. [ALPP Thm A; G15]

## Duality of states and effects

**X6. Weak self-duality.** There is an order isomorphism V_A ≅ V_A^* with
V_A^+ ↦ (V_A^+)^*. "A far less stringent condition than self-duality." [BBLW
§2] Box and even polygons: yes, by a rotation. [BBLW §2; JL §III.A]

**X7. Self-duality (strong).** An inner product on V_A with V_A^+ = (V_A^+)^*;
equivalently an isomorphism Φ : (V_A^+)^* → V_A^+ whose bilinear form
T(e, f) = e(Φ(f)) is symmetric and positive semidefinite. [MU §"Self-duality";
JL Def 2; SSC Def 4.6] Cl_n, Q_N, odd polygons: yes; Box, even polygons: no.
[MU; JL]

**X8. Homogeneity.** Aut(V_A^+) acts transitively on the interior of V_A^+.
[SSC Def 4.2]

**X9. Symmetric cones and Koecher–Vinberg.** Closed, pointed, homogeneous,
self-dual cones are exactly the cones of squares of Euclidean Jordan algebras,
with an appropriate order unit. [SSC Thm 4.8] BH fn 7: the pentagon cone (any
odd n-gon, n > 3) is self-dual but not symmetric, so symmetry is properly
stronger than self-duality.

**X10. Bit symmetry.** Every pair of perfectly distinguishable pure states can
be carried to any other by a reversible transformation. Bit-symmetric ⇒
self-dual, with an inner product nonnegative on states, invariant under
reversible transformations, ⟨ω, ω⟩ = 1 on pure states and ⟨ω, φ⟩ = 0 on
perfectly distinguishable ones; bit symmetry ⇒ transitivity on pure states. In
two dimensions the only bit-symmetric state spaces are the disc and the odd
polygons. [MU Thm 1, remarks]

## Single-system structure

**X11. Perfect distinguishability and frames.** ω₁, …, ω_k perfectly
distinguishable if a measurement has e_i(ω_j) = δ_ij; a frame is a sequence of
perfectly distinguishable pure states; maximal if not extendable; a maximal
frame's sum is the order unit. [BH Def 3.1–3.3, §3]

**X12. Spectrality.** Every ω ∈ Ω_A lies in the convex hull of some frame
("classical decomposability", "every state lies in some classical subspace").
[BH Def 3.4; BMU Postulate 1; SSC Def 4.4]

**X13. Strong symmetry.** Aut Ω_A acts transitively on k-frames for every k
("all classical subspaces of the same size are equivalent"). [BH Def 3.5; BMU
Postulate 2] BH fn 10: the pentagon, and any regular n-gon with n > 3, is
strongly symmetric but not spectral; balls deformed to trivial symmetry are
spectral but not strongly symmetric. BH fn 11: transitivity on 2-frames alone
implies self-duality [their ref. 17].

**X14. Spectral + strongly symmetric = Jordan.** A finite-dimensional compact
convex set is spectral and strongly symmetric iff it is a simplex or the
normalised state space of a simple Euclidean Jordan algebra. Consequences: every
face is generated by a frame, faces have a rank, the face lattice is
orthomodular. If the largest frame has two elements the set is a ball. [BH Thm
1.1, Prop 3.6, Thm 6.1]

**X15. Higher-order interference and the single-system route to quantum
theory.** Postulate 3: no third-order (Sorkin) interference. Postulates 1
(spectrality) and 2 (strong symmetry) imply projectivity (filters for every
face), an orthomodular face lattice and consistent exclusivity; with 3′ (filters
preserve purity) the system is irreducible Jordan-algebraic or classical; with
1–3 the only non-classical theories are real, complex and quaternionic quantum
theory, 3-level octonionic quantum theory and balls; adding observability of
energy gives complex quantum theory. [BMU Postulates 1–3, 3′, Thm 8, 9, 12, 17,
abstract]

**X16. Purification.** [C10]

## Diagrammatic postulates (Selby–Scandolo–Coecke)

**X17. The five postulates.** (1) process theory; (2) finite local classical
interface; (3) cups and caps; (4) sharp dagger; (5) essentially unique
symmetric purification. Chain of lemmas: 2+4+5 ⇒ homogeneous cone; +4 ⇒
spectral; +4 ⇒ strongly self-dual; hence by Koecher–Vinberg systems are
finite-dimensional EJAs; cups and caps then give generalised no-restriction;
purity of cups (equivalently triviality of leaks) restricts to quantum systems.
[SSC Post 1–5, Lemma 4.3, 4.5, 4.7, 4.9, 4.10, Prop 5.1, 5.3] The lesson for
the project: cups and caps are a postulate here, and the causal subtheory never
has them (C6).

## Dualisability and teleportation

**X18. Cups and caps / dualisability.** [C6, C7]

**X19. Conclusive and deterministic teleportation.** [C8; BBLW Thm 1, 3]
Necessary: weak self-duality of the teleported system is what the examples use;
sufficient with a transitive finite group and an equivariant isomorphism.

**X20. Entanglement swapping and teleportation stability.** Theories with
well-defined entanglement swapping; stability of the CHSH value under iterated
swapping classifies into seven families; local dimension ≥ 3 forced. [C13; DLG
Def 1; DG Condition 1, Result 13, Lemma 15]

## Dynamics

**X21. Transitivity and continuous reversibility.** Reversible transformations
carry any pure state to any other (MM Requirement 4); continuously so (Hardy
Axiom 5, dropping it gives classical probability theory). [conditions-catalogue
B]

**X22. Reversible dynamics of boxworld.** Single-system reversible maps of the
gbit are relabellings only; there is no continuous group. [B §VI; MU: the
square is not bit-symmetric]

## Implications recorded in filed sources

- bit symmetry ⇒ self-dual ⇒ weakly self-dual [MU Thm 1; definitions]
- transitivity on 2-frames ⇒ self-dual [BH fn 11]
- spectral + strongly symmetric ⇒ simplex or simple EJA [BH Thm 1.1]
- homogeneous + self-dual ⇔ EJA cone [SSC Thm 4.8]
- SSC postulates 2, 4, 5 ⇒ EJA; + pure cups ⇒ quantum [SSC Lemma 4.9, Prop 5.1]
- cups and caps ⇒ generalised no-restriction [SSC Lemma 4.10]
- BMU 1 + 2 ⇒ projective, orthomodular; + 3′ ⇒ Jordan or classical [BMU Thm 17]
- weakly self-dual + transitive finite group + equivariant V ≅ V^* ⇒
  deterministic teleportation in A ⊗_min (A ⊗_max A) [BBLW Thm 3]
- both cones non-classical ⇔ entangleable [ALPP Thm A]
- 2d GPTFT ⇒ weakly self-dual under a symmetric form (project result R5,
  unverified)
