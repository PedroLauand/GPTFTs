# Toolbox: GPT examples

Explicit theories to test every definition against, in house notation. The
table at the end says which axioms (`gpt-axioms.md`) each satisfies, with the
source; "?" means no filed source settles it; "R" means a project result from
the retired notes, unverified (`project-results-archive.md`).

**E1. Classical Cl_n.** (R^n, R^n_{≥0}, Σ_i). Ω a simplex with n pure states;
effects the vectors in [0,1]^n; the orthant is self-dual; the only composite is
Cl_{nm}; no-broadcasting fails (broadcasting exists). [P Def 4.1, Thm 7.7;
ALPP Def S16; G15]

**E2. Quantum Q_N (complex).** (Herm_N(C), PSD, tr). Ω the density operators;
pure states rank-one projectors; effects 0 ≤ X ≤ 1; PSD self-dual under
Hilbert–Schmidt; frames are lists of orthogonal rank-one projectors, maximal
size N; spectral and strongly symmetric; composite Q_{MN}; no higher-order
interference. [P §8; BH §3 fn 8; BMU]

**E3. Real quantum theory (Herm_N(R)).** Same construction over R. Fails
tomographic locality: the composite has a global sector. Satisfies BMU's
postulates 1–3. [B after Assumption 5; BMU abstract; R12 for the sector]

**E4. Quaternionic and octonionic.** Herm_N(H) for all N; Herm_3(O) only. Both
appear in BMU's classification under postulates 1–3; the exceptional algebra
admits no composites with a non-classical partner. [BMU abstract; BGW Prop 4.14]

**E5. Balls, spin factors.** V = R^n ⊕ R with Ω the unit n-ball: the EJA
spin factors; any strongly symmetric spectral set with maximal frame size 2 is a
ball; ball state spaces of arbitrary dimension pass BMU's postulates 1–3. In
two dimensions the disc is the n → ∞ limit of the polygons. [BH §4 remark, Thm
6.1; BMU abstract; JH §3.5]

**E6. Euclidean Jordan algebras J(A).** Cone of squares, u = tr; primitive
idempotents are the pure states; Jordan frames are the maximal frames; spectral
and strongly symmetric; homogeneous and self-dual (Koecher–Vinberg). Simple EJAs
are Herm_N(R), Herm_N(C), Herm_N(H), spin factors, Herm_3(O). [BH Def 4.1, 4.3,
Prop 4.4–4.7, Cor 4.8; SSC Thm 4.8]

**E7. Boxworld Box (gbit).** The square cone in R³. Not self-dual; weakly
self-dual by a rotation of π/4; not bit-symmetric; single-system dynamics
relabellings only; its composites in Barrett's GNST hold every non-signalling
table; under iterated entanglement swapping a composite of gbits loses its CHSH
value down to 2. The unrestricted convex closure of the Spekkens toy theory.
[P §9; B §IV.C, §VI; MU; BBLW §2; DLG Lemma 3; JL abstract]

**E8. Polygons Poly_n.** Ω = conv{ω_i}, ω_i = (r_n cos(2πi/n), r_n sin(2πi/n),
1) ∈ R³, r_n = (sec(π/n))^{1/2} (formula partly lost in extraction; check).
n = 3 is Cl_3, n = 4 is Box, n → ∞ the disc. Odd n: strongly self-dual; even n:
only weakly, primal and dual matched by a rotation of π/n. Any regular n-gon
with n > 3 is strongly symmetric but not spectral; odd n-gons with n > 3 are
self-dual but not homogeneous. Bit-symmetric iff n odd. [JL (19), §III.A; JH
§3.5; BH fn 7, 10; MU]

**E9. Spekkens toy theory (convex closure).** Described by Janotta–Lal's
generalised maximal tensor product of restricted systems; local; its
unrestricted version is boxworld. [JL abstract, §V]

**E10. Oblate stabilizer theory (OST).** V = Herm(C²); states the cone over six
stretched octahedron vertices x̃_±, ỹ_±, z̃_± with stretch r = 2^{1/4}; effects
the same set rotated by π/4 about z; bipartite cones generated by products and
the R-twisted Bell states (sixteen labels, eight distinct states); higher cones
by Algorithm 2. Not a subtheory of quantum theory (states outside PSD);
CHSH = 4 sustained under iterated swapping; teleportation up to Pauli
corrections. Full definitions, subtleties and derived facts in
`example-ost.md` (labels O1–O13). [DLG Def 4, 5, Lemma 6, Thm 8; candidates
file §1 in R14]

**E11. Dmello–Gross families T_H(a).** V = R^d, d ∈ {4, 6, 8}; effects
primary, local effect cone not generating; bipartite state the Euclidean
pairing; correction groups H ∈ {Z₄, K₄, D₄}; CHSH = 4a stable under iterated
swapping; the Z₄ family is not realisable in quantum theory; local dimension ≥
3 forced. [DG Result 13, Lemma 14, 15; candidates file §2 in R14]

**E12. Density hypercubes DD(fHilb).** Double dilation of quantum theory:
objects DD(H) with states Σ ρ'_{kl} ⊗ conj(ρ'_{kl}); a causal (unique
discarding) theory; third- and fourth-order interference for d ≥ 3, 4; hyper-
decoherence recovers quantum theory in the Karoubi envelope, decoherence
recovers classical theory. [GS-DH Prop 1–3, Def 4; candidates file §3 in R14]

**E13. Nearly quantum composites.** Dagger-compact categories of embedded
Jordan algebras unifying real, complex and quaternionic quantum theory; some
composites fail local tomography, have supermultiplicative capacity, or mixed
states with pure marginals. [NQ abstract, Thm 2]
