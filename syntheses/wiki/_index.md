## The argument in one line

The operational stance puts physics in
[general probabilistic theories](general-probabilistic-theories.md);
[reconstructions](reconstructions-of-quantum-theory.md) select quantum theory
from that landscape by principles; the most famous principle,
[no-signalling](no-signalling.md), is the kinematic half of compatibility with
spacetime; the dynamical half, [locality of action](locality-of-action.md), has
never been imported; importing it leads to
[topological field theory](topological-field-theory.md), and the paper asks
what that requires of a probabilistic theory.
