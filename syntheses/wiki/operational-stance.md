# Operational stance

The draft's starting point [B1+B2, approved 2026-08-31]. Quantum theory is
probabilistic: it assigns probabilities to the outcomes of measurements on
systems, and every empirical claim is a matter of collecting outcome statistics
over repeated runs. Its textbook presentation, complex Hilbert space and
self-adjoint operators, is "an abstract structure whose postulates arguably
carry no direct physical motivation".

The operational view takes "self-evident" features of general laboratory
situations as primitive notions and derives the formalism from simple premises
over them. The payoff, in the draft's words, is that one no longer refers to
the specific mathematical framework of quantum theory but understands it "as a
consequence of natural information-theoretic constraints" within the framework
of [general probabilistic theories](general-probabilistic-theories.md).

## Words

The draft says *postulate* for the Hilbert-space stipulations and *principle*
for a physically motivated requirement. Keep the distinction; see
`conventions/domain/rigour.md`.
