# Reconstructions of quantum theory

[B2b, approved 2026-08-31.] Axiomatizations of quantum theory that "select
quantum theory out of the landscape of GPTs as the unique GPT that satisfies a
set of simple principles". The draft's role model is special relativity:
Einstein derived the Lorentz transformations from the constancy of the speed of
light and the relativity principle, improving the conceptual understanding of
transformations already known. Reconstructions do the same for the quantum
formalism with information-processing assumptions.

Lineage in the draft: Hardy2001 first; CDP2011 and MasanesMuller2011 later.

## Why it matters for this paper

The draft's question [B3] is whether spacetime compatibility supplies further
principles. If it does, the paper is a reconstruction-style result with a
spacetime principle where the others use information-theoretic ones. If
compatibility with a topological field theory structure selects a class rather
than quantum theory, the paper is a classification and B2b's framing needs
revisiting. Standing assumption A3 records this as the largest open
uncertainty.
