# Topological field theory

The draft commits to almost nothing here yet; the toolbox now carries the
standard definitions.

What is fixed in the draft: the working title asks what topological field
theory requires of probabilistic theories; the B7 placeholder says the
low-energy, gapped limit justifies a TFT description and names the
Atiyah–Segal skeleton. The `draft.bib` group for B7 holds Atiyah1988, Segal,
Witten1988, Witten1989, Kitaev2003, LevinWen2005, Nayak2008, Freed2014folklore,
CarquevilleRunkel2018 and Kock2003. None is cited in the text;
CarquevilleRunkel2018 is filed and is the source for
`syntheses/toolbox/tft-framework.md` (T1–T10): bordism categories, TFTs as
symmetric monoidal functors, finite-dimensionality and duality, the 1d
classification by dual pairs, the 2d classification by commutative Frobenius
algebras, and the remark that the 2d classification holds for any symmetric
monoidal target.

Not fixed, deliberately: the bordism category (dimension, orientation, extra
structure), what a TFT valued in GPTs is (the target category, T7, and the
duals it needs, T3), and what the results are. The earlier notes fixed several
of these and were retired on 2026-09-04; they re-enter through `pipeline/` with
their assumptions written down, or not at all. See
`conventions/domain/standing-assumptions.md`, "Things we have decided not to
assume".
